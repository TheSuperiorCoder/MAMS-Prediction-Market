Propose a market

Got a question about something happening at Mass Academy? Suggest it here. An admin will review it, may tweak the wording, and then put it live or explain why not. New to this? How markets work.

{% set default_close = '' %} {% include "_market_fields.html" %}
A good market has…
  • A clear, checkable answer. Anyone reading the rules should reach the same result.
  • A named source for the answer, such as a results page or an announcement.
  • A date, and a rule for what happens if the event is postponed.
  • Nothing private: no grades, health or questions about individual people.
{% include "_creator_stake.html" %}
No proposal fee — just your opening bet. Your opening bet is held until an admin reviews the market. If they approve, it's placed in the new market. If they reject or you withdraw it, your bet comes back. When the market resolves, any leftover pot (up to Ⓜ1,000) goes to you as the creator.
