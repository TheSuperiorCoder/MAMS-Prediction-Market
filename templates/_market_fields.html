Type of market

Stocks over/unders resolve themselves: Over wins if the stock's official close is above the line. The price date defaults to the day betting closes (the next day if betting closes after 4:00 pm).

People bet whether the real number will be over or under the line. Tip: a line ending in .5 (like 50.5) can't be hit exactly, so there's never a tie.
