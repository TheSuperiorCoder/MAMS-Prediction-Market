How it works

1

Pick a market

Each market is a question about a Mass Academy event with two or more possible outcomes.

2

Buy shares

A share at 30% costs about Ⓜ0.30. If that outcome happens, every share pays Ⓜ1. Buying pushes the price up.

3

Sell any time

Changed your mind? Sell shares back before the market closes at the current price.

4

Climb the board

After a result is announced, an admin proposes it and everyone has {{ DISPUTE_HOURS }} hours to dispute it. Then winners are paid. Read the full guide.
